Privacy Policy

Official Policy
Application: PriceIt (priceit.rbnd.cc) • Effective Date: October 29, 2026

At PriceIt (“we”, “our”, or “us”), operating at priceit.rbnd.cc, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard information when you use the PriceIt universal parts procurement and bundle optimization platform.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the minimum amount of data required to provide and secure our services:

  • Google & OAuth Account Information: When you sign in using Google OAuth or other federated identity providers, we collect your name, email address, and avatar URL provided via the openid, email, and profile scopes.
  • Account Credentials: If registering via email, we store a cryptographically hashed version of your password (using bcrypt with work factor 11). If registering with WebAuthn/FIDO2 Passkeys, we store the public credential ID and public key. We never have access to your biometric data or private keys.
  • Workshop & BOM Data: Bills of Materials (BOM), custom part numbers, vendor listings, pricing data, bundle mappings, and purchase orders you explicitly save to your private cloud projects.
  • Technical & Session Information: Operating system / browser User-Agent and IP address for session security, fraud prevention, rate limiting, and device management.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your identity and provide secure access to your saved projects across devices.
  • To execute algorithmic bundle-kit cost solvers and purchase optimization calculations.
  • To send account verification and security alert emails (e.g. password resets or email updates).
  • To protect our platform against brute-force attacks, credential stuffing, and abuse.

3. Google User Data Policy & Limited Use

PriceIt's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We never sell, rent, or trade your Google account data or personal information to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.
  • We do not use your data or project content to train generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning models.
  • We do not transfer your Google user data to any external parties, except as strictly necessary to operate our core service or comply with applicable law.

4. Data Storage, Security & Retention

Your data is stored in isolated, encrypted production databases with strict access controls. Passwords are never stored in plaintext. Session cookies are HTTP-only and transmission is strictly enforced over TLS/HTTPS (TLS 1.2 / 1.3).

5. Account Deletion & Data Rights

You retain complete ownership of your data. You may export your projects to CSV at any time. You can permanently delete your user account and all associated project data directly in the application under Account Settings ➔ Sessions & Danger ➔ Permanently Delete Account.
